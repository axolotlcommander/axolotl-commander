# Quickstart: Drive Row with Free Space

In the test copy of the app (own bundle id), panels on scratchpad folders; reading only.

1. Default settings: each panel shows a drive row below the volume bar with its disk and "X free of Y".
2. Open a folder on another volume (a scratchpad disk image) in one panel: its combo and space change.
3. Click the combo: the volume menu opens below it with the current item checked; pick Home; focus stays
   in the list.
4. Point at the space: the tooltip shows the used percentage. Click it: Volume Info opens.
5. Create a 200 MB file in the scratchpad folder: the free space drops within a few seconds.
6. View → Show Drive Row off: the space moves to the right end of the volume bar. Narrow the window: the
   text shortens and hides before volume buttons.
7. View → Show Volume Bar off: the space is at the right end of the status line.
8. Settings → Appearance → "Show free space when the drive row is hidden" off: the space disappears.
9. Open an SFTP folder or Network: the combo shows the server or Network, no space.
