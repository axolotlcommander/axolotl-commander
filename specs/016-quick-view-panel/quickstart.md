# Quickstart: Quick View Panel

## Automated

`swift build` gives no warnings and `swift test` is green (`QuickViewTests`).

## GUI check (test copy, scratchpad folders, browsing only)

1. Left panel on a test folder with `a.txt`, `b.png`, a subfolder. View → Quick View Panel: the right
   panel shows `a.txt`; the focus stays left.
2. ↓: the preview follows to `b.png`, then the folder; "..": the panel's folder.
3. Enter the subfolder: the preview follows.
4. Tab: the quick view closes, the right panel is active with its folder unchanged.
5. Quick View Panel again, then Maximize Panel: the quick view closes first.
